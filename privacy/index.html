    Privacy policy🔗

    Last updated: 4 May 2026

    This privacy policy explains how data is handled on logan-search.org.

    What we store🔗

    When you submit a query, we store the submitted DNA sequence and its results for 6 months, after which they are automatically deleted. A unique query ID is also saved in your browser's local storage so you can access your query history. We do not collect personal information such as names, IP addresses, or browser fingerprints.

    Email notifications🔗

    If you opt in to email notifications for query results, we use Microsoft Azure's email services to send them. We do not store your email address. Emails are used solely for delivering results, never for marketing or profiling.

    Hosting🔗

    This service is hosted on Microsoft Azure (United States). This means your data is processed on servers located in the US. Azure may log certain technical data (such as IP addresses) as part of its standard infrastructure operations. For details, see the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

    Cookies and tracking🔗

    We do not use cookies, analytics, or any third-party tracking. The only client-side storage is browser local storage for your query history.

    External links🔗

    Our website may link to external sites. We are not responsible for their content, privacy practices, or security.

    Your rights🔗

    You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any data associated with your queries by contacting us at https://github.com/IndexThePlanet/LoganSearch/issues.

    Contact🔗

    For any questions about this policy, reach out at https://github.com/IndexThePlanet/LoganSearch/issues.

    We reserve the right to update this policy at any time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
